🚆 Could a new railway get Ourense–Pontevedra down to 39 minutes? The terrain made me think twice.

For day 8 of #30DayMapChallenge2026, I designed an exploratory rail network for Galicia, in northwest Spain. I wanted to see which new connections might actually change a journey, including the wait for a train and the time spent changing trains.

Ourense–Pontevedra was the route I kept redrawing. My first alignment was about 99 km. After revisiting the approaches, curves and terrain, I brought it down to about 72 km. The model suggests a 39-minute journey. On paper, that looks like a breakthrough.

Then I checked the vertical alignment. In some sections it runs more than 300 m below the terrain. That raises serious questions about construction, cost and environmental impact. I cannot call the route feasible without those studies.

A Coruña–Ferrol gave me a different reality check: even with a new land-based alignment around the estuaries, the model comes out at 35 minutes. My target of under 30 is still out of reach.

I put both the possibilities and the limits into COMPÁS Atlántico: seven proposed connections, new suburban services and an interactive atlas where you can follow trains and compare complete journeys. 🗺️
